About

Interested in the "why," not just the number

I'm a Data Science graduate (M.Sc., University of Europe for Applied Sciences, Germany) looking for my first Data Analyst role. I care less about an impressive-looking metric and more about whether it is the right metric: whether a difference is real or noise, whether a number is measured at the right level, and whether someone can act on what I found.

My thesis tested that idea directly. I built parallel static and interactive explanation pipelines for the same models, then ran a user study to see which one people understood faster and trusted more. The interactive version won on accuracy, speed, and trust. It was a small study, and I say so openly in the thesis.

Alongside my studies I worked as a shift leader, leading a team of 6–8 and training new staff. Based in Halle (Saale), Germany. Open to Data Analyst, BI Analyst and Reporting Analyst roles.
