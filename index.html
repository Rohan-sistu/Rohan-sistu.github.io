About

Interested in the "why," not just the prediction

I'm a Data Science graduate (M.Sc., University of Europe for Applied Sciences, Germany) building toward a Data Analyst role. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and human-centered design: I care less about squeezing out another percentage point of accuracy and more about whether a person can actually trust and act on what a model tells them.

My thesis tested that idea directly. I built parallel static and interactive explanation pipelines for the same models, then ran a user study to see which one people understood faster and trusted more. The interactive version won on accuracy, speed, and trust. It was a small study, and I say so openly in the thesis.

Based in Halle (Saale), Germany. Open to data analyst and ML/analytics roles.
